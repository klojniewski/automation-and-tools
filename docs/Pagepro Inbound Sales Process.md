# **Pagepro Inbound Sales Process**

---

## **Pipeline Stages**

### **1\. Lead In**

**Trigger:** Inbound enquiry via website / contact form

**Actions:**

- Respond within **15 minutes**  
- Goal: book an **Intro Meeting**

**Intro Meeting agenda:**

- Understand the project and client need  
- Share relevant case studies  
- Help the client clarify what they want to achieve  
- Provide ballpark costs and timeline

---

### **2\. Qualification Call Made**

**Trigger:** Intro Meeting completed

**Meeting covers:**

* Understand the project, pain, and goals  
* Share relevant case studies  
* Help the client clarify what they want to achieve  
* Qualify prospect (BANT: Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline)  
* Provide ballpark costs and timeline indication

**Actions:**

- Qualify the prospect (BANT: Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline)  
- Log call transcript  
- Create CRM note with summary and key information  
- Send follow-up email same day (What We Heard / How We Can Help / Next Steps)

**Success criteria:** Call happened \+ CRM note created

---

### **3\. Deal Qualified**

**Trigger:** Qualification criteria met

**Qualified \= all four BANT conditions true:**

- ✅ Budget confirmed  
- ✅ Speaking with a decision maker  
- ✅ Project fits our services and tech stack  
- ✅ Timeline known (start date \+ deadlines)

---

### **4\. Situation Investigated**

**Trigger:** Project Concept Prepared

**Actions:**

- Deep-dive into project requirements and constraints  
- Explore solution options on our side  
- Produce a **Project Concept** document containing:  
  - Current situation (Point A)  
  - Desired outcome (Point B)  
  - Our proposed approach and execution path  
  - More precise ballpark estimate  
- Additional technical calls or async information gathering (calls, emails, meetings) as needed  
- Iterate if needed — **do not proceed to proposal until concept is explicitly approved**

---

### **5\. Concept Confirmed**

**Trigger:** Project Concept presented to client and confirmed by client

**Actions:**

- Present concept to client  
- Get explicit approval before moving forward

**Success criteria:** Client approves the concept and ballpark

---

### **6\. Proposal Sent**

**Trigger:** Proposal sent to client

**Actions:**

- Produce a high-quality, detailed proposal  
- **Preferred:** Present proposal live in a meeting  
- **If no meeting:** Record a walkthrough video explaining the proposal clearly  
- Collect initial feedback and iterate if needed

**Why the video matters:** Forces us to articulate the scope clearly and ensures the client fully understands what they're buying.

---

### **7\. Agreement Sent**

**Trigger:** Proposal accepted by client and agreement sent

**Actions:**

- Send contract  
- Collect feedback, negotiate terms if needed  
- Iterate until contract is approved

---

### **8\. Close \- Won**

**Trigger:** Contract signed by both parties

**Actions:**

- Mark deal as **Won** in CRM  
- Ensure CRM note is complete before handoff  
- Hand off to delivery team

### **❌ Closed Lost**

**Trigger:** Deal exits pipeline at any stage without conversion

**When to mark Closed Lost:**

* Prospect explicitly withdraws  
* No response after maximum follow-ups (see cadence above)  
* Disqualified at any stage (budget, stack, authority, timeline)

**Required:** Log a **loss reason** in CRM — mandatory, no exceptions.
